What Happens at Your First Visit

Your first visit starts with a talk, not a drill. You fill out a short health form, meet the team, and tell them what brings you in. It might be a cleaning you put off for a year, a tooth that hurts when you drink cold water, or a smile you want to feel better about in photos. Then the team checks your teeth and gums, takes low-radiation digital X-rays when needed, and talks with you about what they see. Nothing moves fast, and you get time to ask questions. If you need more work, they lay out the choices, the order, and the cost, so you can think it over at home. A good Dentist Palm Beach Gardens visit should leave you calmer than when you walked in, and that is the goal here.

Dental Care Offered at Steven Weiss DDS

Below is the care you can get at this one office. Each part is short, so you can scan the list, find what you need, and call with questions. Hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Saturday closed, and Sunday by appointment only.

General Dentistry

General dentistry is the day-to-day care that keeps your mouth in good shape. At this office, that means checkups, cleanings, fillings, and help when a tooth starts to hurt, all with a plan that fits your age and your health.

Invisalign

Invisalign uses clear trays that slowly move teeth into a straighter line. Many adults and teens like them because they are hard to spot, they come out for meals, and they do not have metal wires that poke the cheek.

Dental Implants

Dental implants replace a missing tooth with a small post in the jaw and a new tooth on top. They feel steady when you chew, they do not slip like some plates can, and they can last for years with good home care.

Dental Emergencies

Dental emergencies do not wait for a free afternoon. A cracked tooth, a tooth knocked loose, or pain that keeps you up at night gets quick care here, with a calm plan to stop the pain and save the tooth when that is still possible.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry is about how your smile looks and how you feel when you show it. The team talks with you about small changes first, like shade, shape, and spacing, before they talk about bigger steps.

Oral Surgery

Oral surgery covers tooth removal and other care inside the mouth that needs a skilled hand. The team explains each step ahead of time, talks about healing at home, and checks on you after the visit.

Teeth Cleanings

Teeth cleanings sweep away plaque and tartar that a brush at home cannot reach. A cleaning also gives the team a close look at your gums, so small trouble can be caught while it is still easy to fix.

Composite Fillings

Composite fillings fix a small cavity with a tooth-colored material that blends in with the rest of the tooth. The team shapes it to fit your bite, then smooths it so it feels natural when you chew.

Dental Crowns and Caps

Dental crowns and caps cover a weak or broken tooth with a strong shell. They guard the tooth from more cracks, they bring back its shape, and they let you chew on that side again without worry.

Dental Exams

Dental exams are a full look at your teeth, gums, bite, and jaw. The team notes what has changed since your last visit, so they can spot new decay, gum trouble, or wear before it turns into pain.

Fluoride Treatment

Fluoride treatment paints a thin layer on the teeth to help the enamel stay strong. It takes only a few minutes, it can help kids and adults who get cavities often, and it works best with brushing and flossing at home.

Gum Disease Treatment

Gum disease treatment deals with sore, puffy, or bleeding gums. The team cleans below the gum line, talks about home care that helps gums heal, and keeps a close eye on the gums at your next visits.

Oral Cancer Screening

Oral cancer screening is a quick, painless check of the lips, tongue, cheeks, and throat for spots that look odd. It is done during a routine visit, and it gives you peace of mind when the check comes back clear.

Pediatric Dentistry

Pediatric dentistry is dental care made for kids. The team uses a soft voice, short and simple words, and a slow pace, so a child can learn that a dental visit can be a safe and even fun part of growing up.

Periodontal Therapy

Periodontal therapy is care for the gums and the bone that hold your teeth in place. It can mean a deep cleaning, a plan for home care, and return visits to see if the gums are getting firmer and healthier.

Dental Sealants

Dental sealants are a thin coating placed on the back teeth to keep food and germs out of the tiny grooves. They are a big help for kids, and they can help adults who have deep grooves that trap food.

TMJ Treatments

TMJ treatments help when your jaw clicks, aches, or feels stiff when you open wide or chew. The team checks how your bite lines up, talks about habits like clenching, and looks for a fix that eases the ache.

Tooth Extractions Treatments

Tooth extractions treatments are used when a tooth is too hurt to save, or when it crowds the teeth next to it. The team numbs the area, takes the tooth out with care, and gives you clear steps for healing at home.

Bad Breath Treatment

Bad breath treatment starts by finding the cause, which may be gum trouble, a dry mouth, or food stuck between teeth. Once the cause is clear, the team can treat it and share home habits that keep breath fresh.

All on 4 Treatments

All on 4 treatments use four implants to hold a full set of new teeth in one jaw. It can be a good fit for people who have lost most of their teeth and want a steady smile that stays in place all day.

All on 6 Treatments

All on 6 treatments use six implants to hold a full set of new teeth, which can spread the bite over more points. The team checks your jawbone first, then talks about whether four or six posts fit you best.

Single Missing Tooth Replacement

Single missing tooth replacement fills one gap with one implant and one new tooth. It keeps the teeth on each side from drifting into the gap, and it lets you chew and smile without a hole showing.

Multiple Tooth Replacement

Multiple tooth replacement fills a row of gaps with implants and a bridge of new teeth. It can bring back the bite you had before, and it can make it easier to eat foods you had started to skip.

Full Mouth Implants

Full mouth implants rebuild a whole smile for people who have lost most or all of their teeth. The team plans the work in steps, so you know what happens first, what it costs, and how long healing takes.

Dental Implant Crown Repair

Dental implant crown repair fixes a crown on an implant that has chipped, loosened, or cracked. The team checks the post below it too, so the fix lasts and the bite feels even on both sides.

Bone Grafting

Bone grafting adds bone where the jaw has thinned, often so an implant has a firm place to sit. The team explains the healing time in plain words and sets return visits to check how the bone is doing.

Crown Lengthening

Crown lengthening shapes the gum and bone around a tooth so more of the tooth shows. It can help when a crown needs more tooth to hold onto, or when a gummy smile hides teeth you want to show.

Dental Ridge Augmentation

Dental ridge augmentation builds up the ridge of the jaw where a tooth was lost and the bone has sunk. A fuller ridge can make a future implant, bridge, or denture fit and look much better.

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal therapy treats a tooth when the soft core inside it gets hurt or infected. The team cleans out the hurt tissue, seals the tooth, and often tops it with a crown, so you can keep your own tooth.

Wisdom Tooth Removal

Wisdom tooth removal takes out third molars that hurt, crowd other teeth, or grow in at a bad angle. The team talks about pain care, soft foods, and rest, so you know what the next few days will feel like.

Tooth Decay Treatment

Tooth decay treatment stops a cavity from growing and fixes the hole it left. The team removes the soft, hurt part of the tooth, fills it, and talks about habits that can keep new decay from starting.

Toothaches Treatment

Toothaches treatment starts with a close look to find why the tooth hurts, since the cause can be a cavity, a crack, or gum trouble. Once the cause is known, the team can treat it and help the pain fade.

Traumatic Injuries Treatment

Traumatic injuries treatment is care after a fall, a sports hit, or an accident that hurts the mouth. The team checks for loose or broken teeth, treats cuts and swelling, and makes a plan to save the teeth.

Removable Bridges

Removable bridges fill a gap with new teeth that you can take out to clean. They can be a good choice when implants are not the right fit, and the team shapes them to sit snug and feel natural.

Teeth Whitening

Teeth whitening lifts stains from coffee, tea, and time, so teeth look brighter by a few shades. The office uses a safe method that guards your gums, and they tell you how to keep the new shade longer.

Veneers

Veneers are thin shells placed on the front of teeth to change their color, shape, or spacing. They are made to match your other teeth, and they can give a smile a fresh, even look in just a few visits.

Dental Bonding

Dental bonding uses a tooth-colored resin to fix a small chip, close a tiny gap, or smooth a rough edge. It is often done in one visit, with little or no drilling, and it blends in well.

Dentures

Dentures replace a full set of missing teeth, or a full arch, with a plate that fits over the gums. The team takes careful molds, adjusts the fit over a few visits, and shows you how to clean and care for them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I come in for a checkup?

Most people do well with a visit every six months. At that visit, the team cleans your teeth, checks for new trouble, and talks about any changes since your last visit. If you have gum trouble or a lot of decay, they may ask you to come in more often for a while.

What happens during a routine checkup here?

A routine checkup includes a look at your teeth and gums, a cleaning, and X-rays when they are due. The team also checks your bite and your jaw. At the end, they tell you what they found in plain words and what, if anything, needs care next.

Do you see children as well as adults?

Yes, the office sees kids and adults. Kids get a slow, friendly visit that teaches them how to brush and floss, and parents get tips for home care. It is a good way to help a child grow up without fear of the dental chair.

What if I feel nervous about dental visits?

Tell the team when you book, and again when you arrive. They can slow the pace, explain each step before they do it, and give you breaks. Many nervous patients say the hardest part was the first call, and the visit itself felt much calmer than they feared.

Do you take my insurance plan?

Can I get a same-day visit for a toothache?
